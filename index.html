PROOF-OF-LIFE — MARCH AND SEPTEMBER WINDOWS

Prove you're alive. Keep your pension.

Biometric verification runs twice a year — March and September — at any Bank Alfalah branch. Miss a window and the next pension is held, not lost: verify once and it resumes the next month. The September 2026 window has closed, so suspended pensions are this month's real pain.

PENSION CALCULATOR — (AVERAGE WAGE × YEARS) ÷ 50

What should your pension be?

The EOBI formula is (average monthly wage × years of insured service) ÷ 50, with a minimum pension of Rs 11,500 a month. The floor was raised from Rs 10,000 on 1 Jan 2025 — the Rs 1,500 rise over 8 months (Jan–Aug 2025) is Rs 12,000 in arrears. Muhammad Bashir: Rs 45,000 × 22 ÷ 50 = Rs 19,800.

SURVIVORS' GUIDE — WIDOWS KEEP THEIR PENSION

Widows keep their pension.

Fatima Bibi, 64, Lahore — a widow — gets a survivors' pension of Rs 11,500 a month, ACTIVE, credited 3 Nov 2026. A widow's pension continues even if she remarries. If an agent tells you otherwise, he is lying — check the guide, do not take an agent's word.

RED-FLAG SPOTTER

Spot the pension agent.

Four things an agent does that should stop you paying — and one safe habit. An agent asks Rs 2,000–5,000 to "release" your pension; an "agent" wants your CNIC + pension card photocopies and your bank PIN; an SMS / WhatsApp says your pension is blocked unless you pay a fee; a "facilitator" offers to fix missing contribution years for cash. Call each one a red flag or safe, then check your score.
